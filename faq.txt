Q: What does PRN mean?
A: PRN stands for "as needed" and is the same as Per Diem. 
PRN employees follow the same benefits and coverage rules 
as Per Diem employees. Per Diem employees are eligible for 
the Prime Value Plan beginning the first of the month 
following or coinciding with two months from their hire date.
For complete details visit ehp.primehealthcare.com
